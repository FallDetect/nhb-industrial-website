From site study to running system

How we deliver end-to-end automation

Every project starts on your floor and with a business case. You see the payback before you commit.

  1. 01

    On-site study

    We walk your line with your team: process mapping, cycle times, error points, space, utilities and safety constraints.

  2. 02

    ROI analysis

    Labour, scrap, downtime and throughput modelled against investment, giving a clear payback period before you commit.

  3. 03

    Solution design

    Robot cells, IIoT architecture and vision systems specified, simulated and validated against your targets.

  4. 04

    Build and integrate

    Sensors, PLCs, robots and software integrated with your MES, ERP or WMS as one connected system.

  5. 05

    Commission and train

    On-site installation, acceptance testing and hands-on training for operators and maintenance teams.

  6. 06

    Support and optimise

    Preventive maintenance, remote monitoring and data-driven tuning to keep improving performance.

SENSE → CONNECT → DECIDE → ACTEvery action is logged back into the system, so results are measured against the ROI we committed to.
